### HANDPOKE TATTOO (SILENT POINT): PRECISION AND CONTROL.
HANDPOKE TATTOO IS A MANUAL TECHNIQUE WHERE EACH POINT IS APPLIED INDIVIDUALLY, ALLOWING MAXIMUM CONTROL OVER DEPTH AND DETAIL.

- CLEAN AND PRECISE LINEWORK
- IDEAL FOR GEOMATRIC AND FINE LINE TATTOO DESIGNS
- MINIMAL SKIN TRAUMA AND SMOOTH HEALING

THIS METHOD IS PERFECT FOR THOSE LOOKING FOR DETAILED, CONTROLLED, AND REFINED RESULT.

### HAND TAPPING TATTOO (RHYTHMIC TECHNIQUE): BOLD AND TRADITIONAL.
HAND TAPPING TATTOO USES A MANUAL TAPPING METHODE WITH A STICK AND NEEDLE, CREATING A CONSISTENT RHYTHM DURING THE PROCESS

- STRONG AND BOLD PATTERN
- IDEAL FOR TRIBAL TRADITIONAL TATTOO DESIGNS
- UNIQUE TEXTURE AND DEEPER VISUAL IMPACT

THIS TECHNIQUE IS THE BEST FOR LARGE COMPOSITIONS AND CULTURALLY INSPIRED TRADITIONAL TATTOO TRIBAL DESIGNS.
